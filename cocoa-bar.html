🌱 Our Story

A little lounge with a big obsession.

The Cocoa Bar began with a simple idea: the world's two finest indulgences — single-origin cocoa and 100% blue agave tequila — belong on the same table. We roast our beans in-house, temper our chocolate daily, and pour tequilas aged in oak barrels.

Come for the sipping chocolate. Stay for the shots. Leave with a story.

Why cocoa & tequila?

Both are born from ancient Mesoamerican tradition — the Aztecs prized cacao as currency and fermented agave as a sacred drink. Dark chocolate's bitterness softens tequila's fire; agave's sweetness lifts the cocoa's depth. A match 3,000 years in the making.
